# Product Vision

MEMORA is a Personal Memory OS for consumers. It helps people selectively capture information, organize it into a durable personal knowledge base, and retrieve it through answers that show their evidence.

## Promise
Remember what matters, understand where it came from, and retrieve it in context.

## Principles
- Consent before capture.
- Provenance before confidence.
- Temporal context before assumptions.
- User control over retention, export and deletion.
- Provider-independent AI architecture.
